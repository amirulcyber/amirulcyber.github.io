{%- comment -%} One row of the writing list: thumbnail, title and date, then the post's lede. The thumbnail is the post's own `thumbnail:` front matter. It is deliberately not inferred from `image:` — _config.yml sets a site-wide default `image` (the link-preview card) on every post, so `image` is always present and inferring from it would put the brand card on posts that have no artwork of their own. Thumbnails are pre-generated 320x180 cover-crops (`-320.webp` + `-320.jpg`, q70) — the rendered box is 160x90 (112x63 on mobile), so 320w covers 2x DPR while staying ~7KB instead of ~190KB. The lede is truncated in Liquid *and* line-clamped in CSS: the markup stays small, and a browser without -webkit-line-clamp still shows a sane amount instead of the whole paragraph. Usage: {% include essay-card.html post=post %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lede = include.post.description | default: include.post.excerpt -%} {%- assign thumb = include.post.thumbnail -%} {%- if thumb %} {%- assign thumb_base = thumb | split: '?' | first -%} {%- assign thumb_stem = thumb_base | replace: '.jpeg', '' | replace: '.jpg', '' | replace: '.png', '' -%} {%- endif %} {{ include.post.title | smartify }} {{ include.post.date | date: '%d %B %Y' }} {%- if lede %} {{ lede | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | truncatewords: 30 }} {%- endif %}

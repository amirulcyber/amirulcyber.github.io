{%- comment -%} One row of the writing list: title and date, then the post's own lede. The `description` front matter is used rather than Jekyll's generated excerpt, because every post here already carries one written to stand alone — the generated excerpt can otherwise open on a heading, an image or a table. The lede is truncated in Liquid *and* line-clamped in CSS: the markup stays small, and a browser without -webkit-line-clamp still shows a sane amount instead of the whole paragraph. Usage: {% include essay-card.html post=post %} {%- endcomment -%} {{ include.post.title | smartify }} {{ include.post.date | date: '%d %B %Y' }} {%- assign lede = include.post.description | default: include.post.excerpt -%} {%- if lede -%} {{ lede | strip_html | normalize_whitespace | truncatewords: 30 }} {%- endif -%}
